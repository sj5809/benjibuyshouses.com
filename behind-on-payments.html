What You Can Do Today

Whether or not you ever work with us, these steps will help.

  1. Open the mail from your lender, even when it's hard to look at. The dates in those letters matter.
  2. Call your loan servicer and ask about a repayment plan, forbearance, or a loan modification.
  3. Talk to a HUD approved housing counselor. It's free, and they can call your lender with you.
  4. Write down what you owe, what you can afford each month, and what you want to happen with the house.
  5. Reach out early. Every month you wait takes an option off the table.

Common Questions

What People Usually Ask

Will talking to you hurt my credit?+
No. Talking to us, sharing your situation, and getting options has no effect on your credit.
Can I really stay in my house after selling?+
Often, yes. With a sell and stay arrangement you rent the home from us after closing. We'll work out a fair rent with you up front and put it in a written lease.
What if I'm only a month or two behind?+
Even better. The earlier you reach out, the more options you have, including simply working things out with your lender.
