When We're the Better Fit

  • The house needs real work
  • You're behind on payments or facing a deadline
  • You want to stay in the home after selling
  • You can't or don't want to deal with showings
  • You need a sure thing on a set date
📋

When an Agent Is the Better Fit

  • The house is updated and move in ready
  • You have a few months and no pressure
  • Getting the highest price matters most
  • You're comfortable with showings

If that's you, we'll tell you. No hard feelings.
