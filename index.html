Common Questions

You've Got Questions. We've Got Answers.

Still not sure how the process works? Here are the questions we hear most often.

Will I get a fair price for my home?+
We make our offers based on current local market conditions, recent comparable sales, and the condition of your property. Our offer won't be the same as a traditional listing price, because we're paying cash, buying as-is, and covering closing costs, but we never low-ball or play games. Our goal is to make you an offer that makes financial sense for both of us, and we're always willing to explain how we calculated it.
Are there any fees or commissions?+
None. Zero. Zip. When you sell to us, there are no real estate commissions, no hidden fees, and no closing costs for you to pay. The offer we make is the amount you receive at closing. That's what makes the cash buyer process so appealing compared to the traditional route where agents typically take 5 to 6% and you still owe closing costs.
Do I need to make repairs before selling?+
Absolutely not. We buy homes in any condition, and we mean any. Outdated kitchens, bad roofs, foundation cracks, fire damage, hoarding situations, code violations, we've seen it all and we buy it all. You don't need to clean, stage, or fix a single thing before we can make you an offer. We handle everything after we close.
How fast can we actually close?+
We can close in as little as 7 days in most cases. The exact timeline depends on your situation and how quickly the title work can be completed. If you need more time, for packing, finding a new place, or any other reason, that's completely fine too. We'll work around your schedule, whether that's 2 weeks or 2 months.
What if I'm behind on mortgage payments or facing foreclosure?+
This is one of the most common situations we help with. If you're behind on payments or received a foreclosure notice, time is critical, but it's not too late. We can often close quickly enough to stop the foreclosure process and help you walk away with cash instead of losing the home to the bank. Reach out as soon as possible so we can assess your timeline.
Am I obligated to accept the offer?+
Not at all. Submitting your information and even receiving a cash offer from us comes with zero obligation. You're free to think it over, compare options, or simply decide not to sell. We never pressure anyone into a decision. If the offer works for you, great, if not, no hard feelings.
Do you work with inherited or probate properties?+
Yes, we have experience working with inherited homes and properties going through probate. The process can be more complex when an estate is involved, but we've navigated it many times. We work with attorneys and title companies to make sure everything is handled correctly. Reach out and we'll walk you through what's involved in your specific situation.
Are you actually local, or a big out-of-state company?+
We're genuinely local, Northern Kentucky is our home. We're not a hedge fund, a national iBuyer, or a corporation operating from another state. We're a local family that cares about this community, and that's reflected in how we treat every person we work with. When you call or email us, you're talking directly to the people making the decisions, not a call center.
