The Process

Three Steps to a Way Forward

You stay in control the whole time. Nothing happens unless you want it to.

Step 1, Tell Us Your Story

Fill out a short form about the house and what's going on. It takes about a minute.

  • No photos or paperwork needed
  • Any condition, any situation
  • Your information stays private

Step 2, We Talk It Through

We reach out, usually the same day, to understand what you need and walk through your options.

  • You talk to the owners
  • We explain every number
  • Zero pressure, ever

Step 3, You Choose

Stay, sell, or buy it back later. Everything goes in writing and we handle the details.

  • Close through a local title company
  • You pick the timeline
  • We cover the usual closing costs

Common Questions

You've Got Questions. We've Got Answers.

Here are the ones we hear most. There are plenty more on our FAQ page.

Can you really help me keep my house?+
In a lot of cases, yes. With a sell and stay arrangement, we buy the house, pay off the mortgage, and you rent it from us. We can also give you the option to buy it back within two years. Whether it works depends on what you owe and what the house is worth, so we'll look at your numbers and tell you honestly.
Does it cost anything to talk to you?+
No. Reaching out, talking through options, and getting an offer is completely free. You're never obligated to do anything.
I'm behind on payments. Is it too late?+
Usually not. Even after a foreclosure notice there's often time, because foreclosure in Kentucky goes through the courts. The sooner you reach out, the more options you have.
Are you actually local?+
Yes. We live in Northern Kentucky and we run this business ourselves. When you reach out, you're talking to the people who make the decisions.
