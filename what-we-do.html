Step Two

Receive Your Cash Offer

We review your information, research the local market, and come back to you with a written cash offer, typically within 24 hours. We explain our number clearly. There's no pressure to accept and no deadline to respond.

  • Offer in as little as a few hours, almost always within 24
  • Written offer, not a range, not verbal
  • We walk through how we calculated the number
  • Zero pressure, take all the time you need
03
Step Three

Close On Your Timeline. Get Paid.

Accept our offer and we handle all the logistics. We work with a licensed title company. You pick the closing date, 7 days or 90+ days, and receive your cash at closing. We cover all closing costs.

  • Close in as little as 7 days in most cases
  • Or take 30, 60, or 90+ days, your choice
  • We cover all closing costs, nothing deducted from your offer
  • Leave whatever you don't want, we handle cleanout
