How It Works

Here's Exactly How We Help You

No mystery and no pressure. You'll know what's coming at every step, and you can stop at any point. Here's what happens after you reach out.

Step One

Tell Us Your Story

Fill out our short form with the address and a little about what's going on. You don't need photos, paperwork, or a clean house. Just the basics.

The more you tell us, the better we can help. Behind on payments? Want to stay? Need to be out by a certain date? Put it in.

  • No appointment or showing needed
  • Any condition is fine
  • Your information stays private

Step Two

We Look Into It and Reach Out

We check recent sales, public records, and everything you told us. Then one of us reaches out, usually the same day.

This is a conversation, not a sales pitch. We want to understand what you actually need before we suggest anything.

  • You talk to the owners, not a call center
  • Usually within a few hours
  • We'll come see the house if it helps
Step Three

We Walk Through Your Options

Depending on your situation, that could be a sell and stay arrangement, a buy back plan, a cash offer, or honest advice to list with an agent.

We'll explain how we got to every number, and you can ask as many questions as you want.

  • Written offers, not verbal estimates
  • No deadline to decide
  • Bring family or an attorney into it

Step Four

You Choose, We Handle the Rest

If you want to move forward, everything goes in writing. We close through a licensed local title company, and you pick the date.

If you'd rather not, that's the end of it. We won't keep calling.

  • Close in as little as 7 days, or take months
  • We cover the usual closing costs on a cash sale
  • Leave behind what you don't want
