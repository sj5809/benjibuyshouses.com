Four Ways We Help. You Choose What Fits.

Option One

Sell and Stay in Your Home

We buy your house and you keep living in it as a renter. Your mortgage is paid off at closing, the late notices stop, and you don't have to pack up your life.

It works well for people who've fallen behind but can manage a fair monthly rent, or who just need time before a move.

  • You want to stay in the home or the school district
  • The mortgage payment has become too much
  • You need a year or two to get back on track

Option Two

Buy Your Home Back Later

If the house means a lot to you, we can add an option for you to buy it back from us within two years. You stay in the home, rebuild your credit and savings, and then take it back over.

The buy back price is agreed on up front and written into the contract, so you always know the number you're working toward.

  • A job loss or medical bill set you back, but you're recovering
  • You're set on keeping the family home
  • You need breathing room more than a new address
Option Three

Sell for Cash, On Your Terms

The simple route. We make a written offer on the house as it sits. No repairs, no cleaning, no showings, and no commissions. We cover the usual closing costs.

You pick the closing date. Some people want a week. Some want two months. Either is fine, and you can rent back for a bit if you need extra time to move.

  • You're ready to move on and want it done cleanly
  • The house needs work you don't want to take on
  • You value speed and certainty

Option Four

List It, If That's Better for You

If your house is in good shape and you have time, you might come out ahead listing with an agent. When that's true, we'll tell you, even though it means we don't buy it.

Same goes for your lender. If a repayment plan or loan modification is the smartest move, we'll help you understand what to ask for.

  • The house is move in ready
  • You're not under time pressure
  • You're fine with showings and a longer process
A straight word on the details. Not every option works for every house. It depends on what's owed, what the house is worth, and what a fair rent would be. Whatever we offer, you'll get the full terms in writing before you sign anything, and we encourage you to have an attorney or someone you trust look it over.
