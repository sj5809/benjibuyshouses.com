Our Story

Why We Started Benji Buys Houses

It started with a family we knew — good people who had fallen behind on their mortgage after a job loss. They didn't want to call some big company and get a lowball offer from a stranger. They needed someone they could trust.

We helped them sell fast, got them a fair cash offer, and they closed in 11 days. Seeing that family avoid foreclosure and walk away with money in their pocket made us realize this was worth doing full-time.

Northern Kentucky is our home. We grew up here and we care about the people in this community. That means being honest with every homeowner — even when the truth isn't what they hoped to hear.

Since then, we've helped many NKY homeowners in all kinds of situations. We do our best work when the person selling feels good about the deal. That's what keeps us going.
