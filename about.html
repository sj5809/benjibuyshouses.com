Our Story

Why We Started Benji Buys Houses

We kept hearing the same story from people around here. Something went wrong with the house, they called one of those "we buy houses" numbers, and they got a lowball offer from someone who'd never seen the place. Take it or leave it.

That didn't sit right with us. Most people in a tough spot don't need a quick flip. They need someone to listen and lay out their real choices, including ways to keep their home. So that's what we built.

We named the business after our dog, Benji. He's loyal, friendly, and doesn't play games. We try to work the same way.
