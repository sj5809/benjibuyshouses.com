What You Can Do Today

Whether or not you ever work with us, these steps will help.

  1. Pull together your current leases and a list of what each tenant pays.
  2. Note any deposits you're holding. They transfer at closing.
  3. Talk to your accountant about taxes on the sale before you commit.
  4. Give tenants proper notice before any walkthrough.
