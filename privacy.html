Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you give us directly and information collected automatically when you visit our site.

When you submit a form on our website, you may provide us with:

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To communicate with you, We may follow up via email to answer questions or provide updates on our offer.
  • To improve our website, Analytics data helps us understand what's working and what we can do better.

We will not contact you excessively or add you to any unrelated mailing lists. You can opt out of any communication from us at any time by replying to an email with "unsubscribe" or simply letting us know.

Our website may use cookies, small text files stored in your browser, to improve your experience. We use cookies for the following limited purposes:

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones. Doing so may affect the functionality of certain parts of our site, but will not prevent you from submitting a form or contacting us.
