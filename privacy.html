Privacy Policy

Benji Buys Houses ("we," "us") runs benjibuyshouses.com. We will never sell your personal information to data brokers or marketers.

What we collect

When you fill out our form, you may give us your name, email, property address, city, ZIP code, and details about your situation and timeline. You never have to share more than you're comfortable with.

When you visit the site, we also collect basic technical information like your IP address, browser and device type, pages you visit, and campaign information that tells us how you found us.

How we use it

  • To reach out and respond to your request
  • To research your property and prepare options or an offer
  • To follow up by email about your request
  • To understand which ads and pages are working

We won't add you to unrelated mailing lists. If you want us to stop contacting you, just tell us.

Cookies and tracking

We use cookies for analytics and to measure our advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The form will still work.

Outside services

We use HubSpot to store form submissions, and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google for advertising and measurement. These companies have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it for your request, or as the law requires. If we don't end up working together, we may keep it for up to 12 months in case your plans change. You can ask us to delete it anytime.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you. We'll respond within 30 days.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Terms of Use

By using this site, you agree to these terms.

No obligation

Submitting a form doesn't obligate either of us to anything. We aren't required to make an offer, and you aren't required to accept one. Any offer depends on a walkthrough, title review, and a signed agreement.

Not advice

What's on this site is general information, not legal, tax, or financial advice. For decisions about your home, especially sell and stay or buy back arrangements or anything involving foreclosure, please talk with an attorney or a HUD approved housing counselor.

Using the site

Please use the site lawfully, don't submit false information, and don't try to break or scrape it.

Liability

The site is provided as is. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect or consequential damages from using it, and our total liability for any claim about the site is limited to $100.

Governing law

These terms are governed by Kentucky law. Any disputes go to the courts of Kenton County, Kentucky.

Changes

We may update this page. When we do, we'll change the date at the top.

Questions, or want your data deleted? Send us a note and we'll take care of it.
