Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Want to Know About Selling for Cash

We've compiled answers to every question we hear from Northern Kentucky homeowners. Can't find yours? Reach out, we're happy to talk through your situation.

💰The Offer

How our cash offers work
Will I get a fair price for my home?+
We make our offers based on current local market conditions, recent comparable sales in your area, and the current condition of your property. Our offer will generally be lower than what you might see on Zillow or a traditional listing, because we're paying cash, buying as-is, and covering all closing costs, which has real value for sellers who need speed and certainty. We never low-ball or play games, and we're always willing to walk you through exactly how we arrived at our number. If it doesn't make sense for you, there's no obligation to accept it.
How do you calculate your cash offer?+
We look at: (1) the current estimated market value of your home if it were in good condition, (2) the cost of any repairs or updates it needs, (3) our holding costs while we renovate, and (4) the cost of closing. What's left after all of that is what we can offer you. We're transparent about this math, just ask us and we'll break it down for you.
Is the offer written and guaranteed?+
Yes. Every offer we make is put in writing. It's not a verbal estimate or a range, it's a real number we stand behind. In some cases we may want to do a quick walkthrough of the property before finalizing the number, but we'll tell you upfront if that applies to your situation.
Am I obligated to accept the offer?+
Absolutely not. Submitting your info, getting a call from us, and even receiving a written offer all come with zero obligation. You're completely free to think it over, get other offers, or simply decide this isn't the right move for you. We never pressure anyone. If you do want to accept, great, if not, no hard feelings at all.
How long does the offer stay valid?+
Our offers are typically valid for a few weeks, though the exact timeframe can vary based on market conditions. If your offer has expired and you're still interested, just reach out, we're happy to take another look.

📋The Process

What to expect from start to finish
What happens after I fill out the form?+
Once you submit, we review your information and reach out, usually within a few hours, almost always within 24. We'll want to ask a few quick questions about the property and your timeline, and then we'll work on putting together a written cash offer for you. The whole early process is casual and low-pressure, we're just trying to understand your situation so we can give you a fair number.
Do I need to be present for any of this?+
Not necessarily. Many sellers manage the entire process remotely, especially if the home is vacant or an inherited property from out of state. We work with title companies that handle signings electronically or can arrange a mobile notary. If you do need to be present for something, we'll give you plenty of notice and work around your schedule.
How fast can we close?+
We can close in as little as 7 days in most situations. The exact timeline depends on how quickly the title company can complete their work and whether there are any liens or title issues to resolve. If you need more time, to move out, find a new place, settle an estate, that's completely fine. We can work with timelines of 30, 60, or even 90+ days if needed.
What does "closing" actually look like?+
Closing is handled by a licensed title company (not us directly). You'll sign the necessary paperwork, which transfers ownership of the property to us, and you'll receive your proceeds either at the table (check or wire transfer). The title company handles all the legal and financial details to make sure everything is done correctly and securely.
Do I need a real estate attorney?+
Kentucky doesn't legally require a real estate attorney to close a transaction, and most of our sellers don't use one. However, you're always welcome to involve an attorney if you prefer, especially in more complex situations like estates or probate. We have no objection to legal representation and will work cooperatively with your attorney.
What if there are tenants in the property?+
We can often work with occupied properties, including those with tenants. We'll need to know about the tenancy situation upfront, whether there's a lease, how many tenants, and what the lease terms are, so we can factor that into the offer and the timeline. Many landlords sell to us specifically to avoid the headache of managing a tenant-occupied sale.

🔒Fees & Closing Costs

The financial side of selling for cash
Are there any fees or commissions?+
None. Zero. Zip. No real estate commissions (which typically run 5 to 6% in a traditional sale), no hidden fees, no closing costs for you to pay. When we make you an offer, that is the exact amount you walk away with at closing, no deductions, no surprises. This is one of the biggest financial advantages of selling directly to us vs. listing on the market.
Who pays closing costs?+
We do. As the buyer, we cover all typical closing costs, title search, title insurance, settlement fees, and more. This saves you typically 1 to 3% of the sale price compared to a traditional sale where sellers often share these costs.
What about taxes on the sale?+
Any tax implications from the sale of your property are your responsibility, not ours, just as they would be in any real estate transaction. We'd recommend speaking with your accountant or tax advisor about how the sale might affect your taxes, especially regarding capital gains. We can't give tax advice, but we're happy to provide documentation about the sale if needed.
What if I still owe a mortgage on the property?+
That's totally fine and very common. At closing, your mortgage balance gets paid off directly from the sale proceeds before you receive your net amount. As long as your offer is at or above what you owe, you'll walk away with cash. If the property is underwater (you owe more than it's worth), we'd need to explore whether a short sale or other arrangement is possible, reach out and we can discuss your specific situation.

Still have questions about the money side? We're happy to walk through the numbers with you, no pressure.

🏠Your Situation

Common scenarios and specific circumstances
Do I need to make repairs before selling?+
Absolutely not. We buy homes in any condition, and we genuinely mean any. Outdated kitchens and bathrooms, roof damage, foundation issues, fire or water damage, pest infestations, hoarding situations, code violations, we've seen it all and we buy it all. You don't need to clean, stage, do a deep clean, or fix a single thing before we make you an offer or before we close. We handle everything after the sale.
What if I'm behind on mortgage payments or facing foreclosure?+
This is one of the situations where working with us can make the biggest difference. If you're behind on payments or have received a foreclosure notice, time is critical, but it's usually not too late. We can often close quickly enough to stop the foreclosure process entirely, protecting your credit score and giving you cash to move forward. Please reach out as soon as possible so we have time to work with your situation, the earlier you contact us, the more options we have.
Can you help with an inherited or probate property?+
Yes, and we have experience doing it. Inherited properties often come with unique complexities, multiple heirs, outstanding debts, properties in disrepair, or the need to go through probate court before a sale can close. We work with title companies and attorneys who specialize in this, and we can often help even if probate hasn't started yet. Reach out and we'll walk through what's involved in your specific case.
I'm going through a divorce. Can you help?+
Yes. A cash sale is often the cleanest solution for divorcing couples because it removes the most complicated shared asset quickly and with as little ongoing interaction as possible. We can work with both parties, and as long as everyone with ownership agrees to the sale, we can proceed. If there are court orders or attorneys involved, we're happy to work within those constraints.
Do you buy homes that have been vacant for a long time?+
Yes. Vacant homes are often in worse condition due to deferred maintenance, vandalism, or weathering, but that's exactly what we specialize in. We're used to evaluating homes that have been sitting empty and making fair offers on them. If you have a vacant property you've been wanting to get rid of, please reach out.
What if my home is in very bad condition?+
We've bought homes in every imaginable condition. Fire damage, black mold, collapsed ceilings, structural issues, unsafe conditions, none of these are automatic disqualifiers. The condition will obviously factor into the offer price, but we don't walk away just because a property is rough. Fill out the form and tell us what you're working with, there's no harm in asking.
Are you actually a local company, or a large out-of-state corporation?+
We're genuinely local. Northern Kentucky is our home, it's where we live and work. We're not a big national company or a call center in another state. When you contact us, you're talking directly to the person making decisions about your property, not a sales rep following a script. That personal connection matters to us, and we think it shows.
What areas of Northern Kentucky do you cover?+
We buy homes throughout the greater Northern Kentucky region, including Covington, Newport, Florence, Erlanger, Independence, Hebron, Burlington, Fort Mitchell, Fort Thomas, Bellevue, Dayton, Highland Heights, Cold Spring, Alexandria, and many more communities. If you're not sure whether we cover your area, just ask, we cover more ground than you might expect.
